# 📊 Pick Your Goal, Build Your Skills
### The Data Analyst Career Guide for Career Pivoters

> *Which industries and skills give new data analysts the best entry opportunities, highest starting pay, and strongest salary growth potential?*

A data analysis project completed as part of the **Eskwelabs Data Analytics Bootcamp** (Sprint 3 Final Output, Group 4).

## 📌 Project Overview

Data analytics is one of the fastest-growing and most accessible career fields for career pivoters — but not all industries offer the same opportunities. This project analyzes a real-world dataset of Data Analyst job postings to answer:

- Which industries have the highest demand for Data Analysts?
- Which industries offer the most accessible entry points for career pivoters?
- Which industries pay the highest average entry-level salaries?
- Which industries show the strongest long-term salary growth?
- What skills are most in-demand within each top industry?
- How much experience is typically needed to move from Junior to Senior?

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries

- **Python** — pandas, numpy
- **Data Visualization** — matplotlib, seaborn
- **Environment** — Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## 🧹 Process

1. **Data Cleaning** — standardized column names, filtered to Data Analyst roles, parsed salary ranges, categorized company size, seniority level, and years of experience required.
2. **Feature Engineering** — extracted skill mentions (SQL, Python, Excel, Tableau, Statistics, SAS, R, etc.) from job descriptions using pattern matching.
3. **Exploratory Analysis** — compared industries by job volume, entry-level accessibility, average salary, salary growth, and skill demand.
4. **Insights & Recommendations** — synthesized findings into a career guide for people pivoting into data analytics.

## 🔑 Key Findings

- **IT Services** and **Staffing & Outsourcing** account for nearly 50% of job postings among the top 5 industries — the most accessible entry points by volume.
- **Aerospace & Defense** offers the highest average entry-level salary (~$92,000).
- **Real Estate** and **Logistics & Supply Chain** show the strongest long-term salary growth, with senior pay 67–69% higher than junior pay.
- **State & Regional Agencies** and **Real Estate** require the least experience (~2.6–3 years) to move from Junior to Senior.
- Across most industries, **SQL** and **Excel** are the foundational skills, with Python, Statistics, and Tableau as secondary differentiators.
